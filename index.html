How it plays

Your satellite circles a planet. Tap and it flies off on a tangent — the line it was already travelling. Time it so that line passes through the middle of the next orbit and you are caught. Pass close to the centre for a PERFECT and the combo climbs. Miss, and you drift off the screen.

What is in it

  • Ranks. One hundred of them, from Rookie Navigator to Primordial Navigator V at 1,900 hops.
  • A hangar of moons. One hundred satellites, each with its own generated surface — craters, ice, lava, canyons, storms. They change how your satellite looks and nothing else: no moon makes the game easier.
  • Daily missions and stars. Five missions a day, and stars you spend in the hangar.
  • Twenty languages, including right-to-left Arabic. English by default, chosen on first launch and changeable in Settings.
  • Plays offline. Everything except watching an ad works with no connection at all.

Ads, plainly

The game is free and carries ads from Google AdMob: a banner, and rewarded video you choose to watch to continue a run or collect stars. Nothing in the game is locked behind an ad — declining one only means you do not get that reward.

No ads appear during the first five launches, and none ever interrupt a run. What Google receives when an ad is shown is set out in the privacy policy.
